# Smoking Policy

## Smoking in Rental Vehicles
All vehicles in our fleet are strictly non-smoking. Smoking inside any rental vehicle is prohibited.

## Penalty
If a vehicle is returned with evidence of smoking (smoke odor, ash, burn marks), a cleaning fee of $250 will be charged. The customer may also be charged for any damage caused by smoking, including replacement of seat fabric and headliners.

## Vaping and E-Cigarettes
The use of electronic cigarettes and vaping devices is also prohibited inside rental vehicles. The same $250 cleaning fee applies.
