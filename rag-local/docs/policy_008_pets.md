# Pet Policy

## Pets in Rental Vehicles
Pets are permitted in rental vehicles under the following conditions:
- The vehicle must be returned clean and free of pet hair, dander, and any damage caused by the pet.
- A pet cleaning fee of $100 will be charged if the vehicle requires additional cleaning after return.
- Pets must be secured in a pet carrier or pet restraint system while the vehicle is in motion.

## Service Animals
Service animals are exempt from the pet policy. No additional fees apply. Customers are not required to provide documentation for service animals under applicable law.
