# Corporate Account Policy

## Eligibility
Corporate accounts are available to registered businesses, government agencies, and non-profit organizations with a valid tax ID number.

## Benefits
- Consolidated monthly billing.
- Discounted rates based on volume.
- Dedicated account manager.
- Access to online account management portal.
- Ability to authorize multiple employees as drivers.

## Application Process
1. Complete the corporate account application form.
2. Provide business registration documents and tax ID.
3. Provide a list of authorized drivers.
4. Provide a preferred payment method.
5. Submit for credit review (approval typically within 3-5 business days).

## Liability
The corporate entity is financially responsible for all rentals made under the corporate account, including damage, traffic violations, and late fees, regardless of which authorized driver was operating the vehicle.
