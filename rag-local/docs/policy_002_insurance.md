# Insurance and Protection Products

## Overview
Customers may purchase protection products at the time of rental to reduce their financial responsibility in case of damage or loss.

## Available Products
- **Damage Waiver (DW)**: Waives financial responsibility for damage to the rental vehicle, subject to the terms of the waiver. Cost: $15/day.
- **Liability Protection (LP)**: Provides third-party liability coverage up to $1,000,000. Cost: $10/day.
- **Personal Accident Insurance (PAI)**: Covers medical costs for the driver and passengers in case of an accident. Cost: $5/day.
- **Personal Effects Coverage (PEC)**: Covers loss or damage to personal belongings up to $500. Cost: $3/day.

## Important Notes
- Protection products must be purchased before the vehicle leaves the lot.
- Protection products are non-refundable once the rental period begins.
- Customers with personal auto insurance may already have coverage. It is the customer's responsibility to verify.
- Protection products do not cover: intentional damage, damage from off-road driving, theft due to negligence (e.g., leaving keys in the vehicle).
