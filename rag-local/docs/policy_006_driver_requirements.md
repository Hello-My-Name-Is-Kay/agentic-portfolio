# Driver Requirements

## Minimum Age
- Drivers must be at least 21 years of age.
- Drivers aged 21-24 will be charged a young driver surcharge of $20/day.
- Drivers aged 25 and above pay the standard rate.

## Valid Driver's License
- All drivers must possess a valid, unexpired driver's license issued by a recognized government authority.
- International driver's licenses are accepted for up to 90 days. After 90 days, a local license is required.
- If the license is in a language other than English, an International Driving Permit (IDP) is required in addition to the original license.

## Additional Drivers
- Additional drivers may be added to the rental agreement.
- All additional drivers must be present at the time of pickup to sign the agreement.
- An additional driver fee of $10/day applies for each extra driver.
- All additional drivers must meet the same age and license requirements as the primary renter.
