# Rental Agreement Policy

## Overview
All vehicle rentals are governed by a standard Rental Agreement signed at the time of pickup. The agreement outlines the rights and responsibilities of both the rental company and the customer.

## Key Terms
- **Rental Period**: The rental begins at the scheduled pickup time and ends when the vehicle is returned to any authorized return location.
- **Authorized Drivers**: Only the customer who signed the agreement and any additional drivers listed on the agreement are authorized to operate the vehicle.
- **Vehicle Condition**: The customer must inspect the vehicle before accepting it. Any pre-existing damage must be documented on the pickup form.
- **Use of Vehicle**: The vehicle may not be used for illegal purposes, to tow trailers or other vehicles unless expressly permitted, or in any way that violates the terms of this agreement.

## Liability
The renter is financially responsible for any damage to the vehicle or third-party property during the rental period, subject to the protection products purchased at the time of rental.

## Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement at any time. Upon termination, the vehicle must be returned immediately to the nearest authorized return location.
