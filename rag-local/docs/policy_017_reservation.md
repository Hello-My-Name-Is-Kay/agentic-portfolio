# Reservation Policy

## Making a Reservation
Reservations can be made online, by phone, or in person at any rental location. A valid credit or debit card is required to secure the reservation.

## Reservation Confirmation
Upon making a reservation, the customer will receive a confirmation email with:
- Reservation number
- Pickup location, date, and time
- Return location, date, and time (if different)
- Vehicle category reserved
- Estimated total cost
- Confirmation of any protection products or additional services selected

## Modifying a Reservation
Reservations may be modified up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time, subject to availability. Changes to the rental location, vehicle category, or rental duration may affect the total cost.

## Reservation Guarantee
We guarantee that your reserved vehicle category will be available at the time of pickup. If the reserved category is not available, we will upgrade you to the next available category at no additional cost.

## Overbooking
In the rare event that no vehicle is available at the time of pickup, we will:
1. Arrange alternative transportation at our expense.
2. Provide compensation equal to one day's rental.
3. Offer priority booking for the next available vehicle.
