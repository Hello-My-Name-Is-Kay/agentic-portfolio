# Late Return Policy

## Grace Period
A 30-minute grace period is provided after the scheduled return time. No late fees apply within the grace period.

## Late Fees
- 31 minutes to 3 hours late: charged 50% of the daily rental rate.
- 3 to 6 hours late: charged 100% of the daily rental rate.
- 6 to 24 hours late: charged 150% of the daily rental rate.
- More than 24 hours late: charged 200% of the daily rental rate plus the vehicle is considered stolen and police may be notified.

## Extension Requests
If you know you will be late, call the rental office as soon as possible. Extension requests made before the scheduled return time may be approved at the standard daily rate. Late fees apply only if the extension is not pre-approved.
