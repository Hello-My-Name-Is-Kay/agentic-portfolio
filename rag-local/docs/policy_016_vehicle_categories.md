# Vehicle Categories and Features

## Economy Class
- Small, fuel-efficient vehicles
- Ideal for city driving and short trips
- Seats up to 4 passengers
- Examples: Sedan, Compact Car

## Standard Class
- Mid-size vehicles with ample luggage space
- Suitable for family trips and longer journeys
- Seats up to 5 passengers
- Examples: Mid-size Sedan, Standard Car

## Premium Class
- Luxury vehicles with enhanced features
- GPS navigation, premium sound system, leather seats
- Seats up to 5 passengers
- Minimum age requirement: 25 years
- Examples: Luxury Sedan, Premium Car

## SUV and Utility Class
- Larger vehicles for cargo or rough terrain
- 4-wheel drive available on select models
- Seats up to 8 passengers
- Higher fuel consumption
- Examples: SUV, Utility Vehicle

## Van and Minibus Class
- Transport for larger groups
- Seats 8-15 passengers
- May require a special driver's license endorsement
- Examples: Passenger Van, Minibus
