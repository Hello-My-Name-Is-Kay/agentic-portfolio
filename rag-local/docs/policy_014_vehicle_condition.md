# Vehicle Condition and Returns

## Pre-Rental Inspection
The customer and rental agent will jointly inspect the vehicle before the rental begins. The inspection covers:
- Exterior damage (dents, scratches, chips, cracks)
- Interior condition (cleanliness, upholstery condition)
- Tire condition and pressure
- Fluid levels
- Fuel level
- Mileage reading

## Return Inspection
Upon return, a rental agent will inspect the vehicle for new damage. The customer has the right to be present during the return inspection.

## Damage Documentation
All pre-existing damage is documented on the pickup form with photos and diagrams. The customer signs the pickup form to acknowledge the documented condition.

## Disputes
If the customer disagrees with the damage assessment at return, they may request a review within 7 days by providing photographic evidence taken at the time of return.
