# Additional Services

## GPS Navigation
- Available for most vehicle categories.
- Cost: $8/day.
- Included free with Premium and above categories.

## Child Safety Seats
- Available upon request.
- Cost: $12/day.
- Types available: Infant (0-12 months), Toddler (1-3 years), Booster (4-7 years).

## Snow Chains
- Available for vehicles during winter months (November through March).
- Cost: $15/day.
- Customer is responsible for proper installation.

## Wi-Fi Hotspot
- Portable Wi-Fi hotspot.
- Cost: $10/day.
- Includes 1GB of data per day. Additional data: $5/GB.

## Ski Rack
- Available for Standard and above vehicle categories.
- Cost: $10/day.
- Maximum capacity: 4 pairs of skis or 2 snowboards.
