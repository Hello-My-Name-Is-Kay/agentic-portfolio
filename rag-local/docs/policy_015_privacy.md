# Privacy Policy

## Information Collected
We collect and process the following personal information:
- Name, address, and contact details
- Driver's license information
- Payment card information (processed through PCI-compliant systems)
- Rental history and driving records
- Location data during the rental period (via GPS tracking where applicable)

## Use of Information
Personal information is used for:
- Processing and managing reservations
- Billing and payment processing
- Customer service and support
- Compliance with legal and regulatory requirements
- Fraud prevention and detection

## Data Sharing
We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with:
- Law enforcement agencies (upon valid legal request)
- Insurance providers (for claims processing)
- Authorized service providers (for roadside assistance, vehicle maintenance)

## Data Retention
Personal information is retained for the duration required by applicable law and our data retention policy, after which it is securely deleted or anonymized.

## Customer Rights
Customers may request access to their personal information or request correction of inaccurate information by contacting our privacy office.
