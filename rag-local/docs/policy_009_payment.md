# Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
- Major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards (with funds authorization hold)
- Cash (not accepted at all locations; check with specific location)

## Authorization Hold
At the time of pickup, an authorization hold will be placed on the customer's credit or debit card. This hold is not a charge but a temporary reservation of funds:
- Standard hold: $200 plus estimated rental charges.
- The hold is released upon return of the vehicle. Release may take 3-5 business days depending on the issuing bank.

## Prepaid Rentals
Rentals paid in full at the time of booking are considered prepaid. Prepaid rentals are non-refundable unless canceled within the free cancellation period (see Cancellation Policy).

## Deposits
A deposit may be required for customers without a major credit card, customers renting certain vehicle categories, or customers with insufficient credit history. The deposit amount varies but is generally equal to the estimated total rental cost plus $200.
