# Customer Identification Requirements

## At Pickup
The customer must present a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID at the time of pickup. Acceptable forms of identification include:
- Driver's license
- Passport
- National identity card

## Verification Process
The rental agent will verify that:
1. The name on the ID matches the name on the reservation.
2. The ID is not expired.
3. The ID appears genuine and unaltered.
4. For corporate rentals, the individual picking up the vehicle is listed as an authorized driver on the corporate account.

## Failure to Provide ID
If the customer cannot provide acceptable identification, the rental will not be processed and the reservation may be subject to the late cancellation policy.
