# Dispute Resolution

## Filing a Dispute
If a customer wishes to dispute any charge or damage assessment, they must:
1. Contact the rental office where the rental occurred within 7 days of the rental return.
2. Provide a written description of the dispute along with any supporting evidence.
3. A dispute resolution specialist will respond within 5 business days.

## Escalation
If the dispute is not resolved to the customer's satisfaction, the customer may escalate to:
- The Regional Customer Service Manager (for disputes under $500).
- The Corporate Customer Relations Department (for disputes over $500).

## Mediation
For disputes that remain unresolved after escalation, the customer may request third-party mediation. The cost of mediation is shared equally by the rental company and the customer unless the mediator determines otherwise.

## Legal Action
Nothing in this policy prevents the customer from seeking legal remedies available under applicable law.
