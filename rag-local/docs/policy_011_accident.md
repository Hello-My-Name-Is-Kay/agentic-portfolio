# Accident and Incident Reporting

## In Case of an Accident
1. Ensure everyone's safety. Call emergency services (911) if anyone is injured.
2. Do not admit fault or agree to pay for damages at the scene.
3. Exchange insurance and contact information with all involved parties.
4. Document the scene: take photos of all vehicles involved, the surrounding area, and any visible damage.
5. Call the rental office at 1-800-RENT-HELP as soon as possible to report the incident.

## Police Report
If the accident involves injury, damage to third-party property, or any dispute about fault, a police report must be filed. Obtain the police report number and the responding officer's name and badge number.

## After-Hours Reporting
If the accident occurs outside business hours, leave a voicemail at the roadside assistance number with your reservation number, location, and a brief description. A representative will return your call within 30 minutes.

## Failure to Report
Failure to report an accident within 24 hours may result in voiding of protection products purchased and full financial responsibility for all damages.
