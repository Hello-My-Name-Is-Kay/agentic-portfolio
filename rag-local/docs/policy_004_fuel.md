# Fuel Policy

## Full-to-Full Policy
All vehicles are provided with a full tank of fuel. The customer must return the vehicle with a full tank of fuel. If the vehicle is returned with less than a full tank, the customer will be charged for the fuel plus a refueling service fee.

## Refueling Service Fee
If the customer does not refuel before returning, a refueling service fee of $25 plus the cost of fuel will be charged.

## Type of Fuel
Customers must use the correct fuel type for the vehicle. Using incorrect fuel may result in engine damage, for which the customer will be held fully responsible. The correct fuel type is indicated on the fuel cap and in the vehicle manual.
