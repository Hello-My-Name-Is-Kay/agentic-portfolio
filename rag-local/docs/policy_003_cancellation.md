# Cancellation Policy

## Free Cancellation Period
Customers may cancel a reservation free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled pickup time.

## Late Cancellation
Cancellations made less than 24 hours before pickup will incur a fee equal to one day's rental cost.

## No-Show
If the customer does not arrive within 2 hours of the scheduled pickup time and has not contacted the rental office, the reservation will be marked as a no-show and the customer will be charged one day's rental.

## Extension Policy
Customers wishing to extend their rental must contact the rental office before the scheduled return time. Extensions are subject to vehicle availability. A late return without prior approval may result in additional fees equal to 150% of the daily rental rate for each additional day.
