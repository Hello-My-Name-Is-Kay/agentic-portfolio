# Mileage Policy

## Unlimited Mileage
Most vehicles in our fleet come with unlimited mileage. No additional charges apply regardless of how far the vehicle is driven.

## Limited Mileage Vehicles
Certain vehicle categories (luxury, specialty, and oversized vehicles) may have a daily mileage limit. The limit will be disclosed at the time of booking.

- **Standard daily limit**: 150 miles per day.
- **Overage charge**: $0.35 per mile over the limit.

## Long-Distance Travel
Customers traveling across state lines must notify the rental office at the time of pickup. Some vehicles are restricted from traveling outside the state. International travel is strictly prohibited unless prior written authorization has been obtained.
