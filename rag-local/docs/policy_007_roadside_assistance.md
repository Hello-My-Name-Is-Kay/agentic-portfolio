# Roadside Assistance

## Coverage
Roadside assistance is available 24/7 for all rental vehicles. Services include:
- Flat tire changes
- Battery jump-starts
- Lockout assistance
- Fuel delivery (customer pays for fuel)
- Towing to the nearest authorized repair facility

## How to Request Assistance
Call the roadside assistance hotline at 1-800-RENT-HELP. Provide your reservation number and location. Estimated response time is 30-45 minutes in urban areas and 60-90 minutes in rural areas.

## Cost
Roadside assistance is included free of charge if you purchased the Damage Waiver or Roadside Assistance Package at the time of rental. Otherwise, a per-incident fee of $75 applies.
